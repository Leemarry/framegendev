Preserve the existing product's restrained official 0.1.17 style.

Keep all images visually related while varying angle, crop, and lighting. Add a compact line “已生成 4 张 · 当前采用 1 张”.
Style/medium: high-fidelity product UI mockup, realistic 2D interface screenshot, refined Chinese SaaS/editorial design, not a marketing landing page, no decorative illustration.

Lighting/mood: calm, professional, focused, practical.
Color palette: white, very light cool gray, charcoal navy text, muted blue selection and primary actions, subtle green accent only for the jade object. Avoid a one-color purple theme.

Text (verbatim): “道具设置”, “翠绿救命丹药”, “角色”, “场景”, “道具”, “分镜”, “图片候选”, “生成图片”, “重新生成”, “4 张”, “保留历史候选”, “主图”, “设为主图”, “查看大图”, “删除候选”, “已生成 4 张 · 当前采用 1 张”.
